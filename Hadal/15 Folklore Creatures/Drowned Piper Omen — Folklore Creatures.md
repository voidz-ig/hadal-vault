# Drowned Piper Omen — Folklore Creatures

**Concept:** Drowned Piper Omen — Folklore Creatures is a small Hadal detail within the Folklore Creatures category. It exists to support entities filtered through local stories, rituals and contradictory eyewitness accounts.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **moderate**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **crew comparison of conflicting evidence**. The intended response is usually to **split responsibilities**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Progression value
