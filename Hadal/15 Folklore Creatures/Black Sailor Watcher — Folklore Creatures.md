# Black Sailor Watcher — Folklore Creatures

**Concept:** Black Sailor Watcher — Folklore Creatures is a major Hadal detail within the Folklore Creatures category. It exists to support entities filtered through local stories, rituals and contradictory eyewitness accounts.

## Progression value
