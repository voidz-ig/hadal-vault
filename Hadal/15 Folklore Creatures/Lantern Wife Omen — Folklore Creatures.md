# Lantern Wife Omen — Folklore Creatures

**Concept:** Lantern Wife Omen — Folklore Creatures is a medium Hadal detail within the Folklore Creatures category. It exists to support entities filtered through local stories, rituals and contradictory eyewitness accounts.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **deceptively minor**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **crew comparison of conflicting evidence**. The intended response is usually to **communicate a confirmation phrase**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a brief absence of ordinary ocean noise. It can leave evidence that persists back in town.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **new dialogue and rumors**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
