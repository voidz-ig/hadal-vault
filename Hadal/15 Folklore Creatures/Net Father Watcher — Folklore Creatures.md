# Net Father Watcher — Folklore Creatures

**Concept:** Net Father Watcher — Folklore Creatures is a small Hadal detail within the Folklore Creatures category. It exists to support entities filtered through local stories, rituals and contradictory eyewitness accounts.

A recurring tell is light scattering that looks slightly delayed. It becomes more dangerous when players stop communicating.
