# Wake Child Watcher — Folklore Creatures

**Concept:** Wake Child Watcher — Folklore Creatures is a medium Hadal detail within the Folklore Creatures category. It exists to support entities filtered through local stories, rituals and contradictory eyewitness accounts.

Its baseline pressure is **moderate**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **slow down and verify**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Progression value
