# Dragged Shrine — Environmental Storytelling

**Concept:** Dragged Shrine — Environmental Storytelling is a small Hadal detail within the Environmental Storytelling category. It exists to support small environmental details that imply human events without stopping play for exposition.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **rare but severe**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **communicate a confirmation phrase**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a brief absence of ordinary ocean noise. It becomes more dangerous when players stop communicating.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **equipment access**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
