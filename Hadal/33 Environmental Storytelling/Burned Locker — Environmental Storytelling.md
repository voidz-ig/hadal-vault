# Burned Locker — Environmental Storytelling

**Concept:** Burned Locker — Environmental Storytelling is a small Hadal detail within the Environmental Storytelling category. It exists to support small environmental details that imply human events without stopping play for exposition.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **deceptively minor**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sonar or instrument drift**. The intended response is usually to **communicate a confirmation phrase**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is light scattering that looks slightly delayed. It is easier to survive than to correctly understand.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **research value**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
