# Flooded Locker — Environmental Storytelling

**Concept:** Flooded Locker — Environmental Storytelling is a small Hadal detail within the Environmental Storytelling category. It exists to support small environmental details that imply human events without stopping play for exposition.

Its baseline pressure is **rare but severe**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sound pattern recognition**. The intended response is usually to **split responsibilities**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

A recurring tell is sediment moving against the current. It becomes more dangerous when players stop communicating.
