# Signal Hook — Surface Equipment

**Concept:** Signal Hook — Surface Equipment is a major Hadal detail within the Surface Equipment category. It exists to support sailor tools for observation, rescue, deterrence and crisis response.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **deceptively minor**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **communicate a confirmation phrase**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is light scattering that looks slightly delayed. It can leave evidence that persists back in town.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **money and town recovery**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
