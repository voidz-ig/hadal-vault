# Palecrest Ray — Natural Creatures

**Concept:** Palecrest Ray — Natural Creatures is a medium Hadal detail within the Natural Creatures category. It exists to support plausibly biological sea life that anchors early danger before the impossible becomes undeniable.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **high under bad conditions**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sound pattern recognition**. The intended response is usually to **split responsibilities**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is radio static with a human cadence. Experienced crews notice it earlier but may overreact.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **new dialogue and rumors**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
