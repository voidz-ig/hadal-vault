# Palecrest Stalker — Natural Creatures

**Concept:** Palecrest Stalker — Natural Creatures is a small Hadal detail within the Natural Creatures category. It exists to support plausibly biological sea life that anchors early danger before the impossible becomes undeniable.

## Progression value
