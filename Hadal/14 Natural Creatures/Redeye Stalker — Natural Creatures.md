# Redeye Stalker — Natural Creatures

**Concept:** Redeye Stalker — Natural Creatures is a major Hadal detail within the Natural Creatures category. It exists to support plausibly biological sea life that anchors early danger before the impossible becomes undeniable.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **deceptively minor**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sound pattern recognition**. The intended response is usually to **retreat before escalation**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is light scattering that looks slightly delayed. Experienced crews notice it earlier but may overreact.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **safer future routes**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
