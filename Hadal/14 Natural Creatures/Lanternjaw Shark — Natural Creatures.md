# Lanternjaw Shark — Natural Creatures

**Concept:** Lanternjaw Shark — Natural Creatures is a small Hadal detail within the Natural Creatures category. It exists to support plausibly biological sea life that anchors early danger before the impossible becomes undeniable.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **rare but severe**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **visual observation**. The intended response is usually to **split responsibilities**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is radio static with a human cadence. It is easier to survive than to correctly understand.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **rare crafting material**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
