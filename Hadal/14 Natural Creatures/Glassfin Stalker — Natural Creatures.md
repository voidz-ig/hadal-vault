# Glassfin Stalker — Natural Creatures

**Concept:** Glassfin Stalker — Natural Creatures is a small Hadal detail within the Natural Creatures category. It exists to support plausibly biological sea life that anchors early danger before the impossible becomes undeniable.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **moderate**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sonar or instrument drift**. The intended response is usually to **slow down and verify**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is radio static with a human cadence. It becomes more dangerous when players stop communicating.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **money and town recovery**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
