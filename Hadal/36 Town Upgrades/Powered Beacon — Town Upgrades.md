# Powered Beacon — Town Upgrades

**Concept:** Powered Beacon — Town Upgrades is a small Hadal detail within the Town Upgrades category. It exists to support visible town improvements that convert expedition loot into a changing home base.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **rare but severe**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sonar or instrument drift**. The intended response is usually to **split responsibilities**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a smell of wet iron. It is easier to survive than to correctly understand.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **money and town recovery**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
