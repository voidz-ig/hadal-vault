# Research Beacon — Salvage

**Concept:** Research Beacon — Salvage is a small Hadal detail within the Salvage category. It exists to support recoverable human-made objects that tell small stories and create inventory decisions.

Its baseline pressure is **high under bad conditions**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **visual observation**. The intended response is usually to **split responsibilities**, rather than treating every problem as combat.
