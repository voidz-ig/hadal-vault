# Crushed Engine — Salvage

**Concept:** Crushed Engine — Salvage is a small Hadal detail within the Salvage category. It exists to support recoverable human-made objects that tell small stories and create inventory decisions.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **deceptively minor**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **visual observation**. The intended response is usually to **slow down and verify**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is sediment moving against the current. It can leave evidence that persists back in town.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **equipment access**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
