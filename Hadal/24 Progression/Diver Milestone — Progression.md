# Diver Milestone — Progression

**Concept:** Diver Milestone — Progression is a small Hadal detail within the Progression category. It exists to support long-term progression that broadens choices instead of simply flattening danger.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **high under bad conditions**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **visual observation**. The intended response is usually to **spend a limited tool charge**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is sediment moving against the current. It can leave evidence that persists back in town.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **rare crafting material**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
