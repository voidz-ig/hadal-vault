# Reputation Threshold — Progression

**Concept:** Reputation Threshold — Progression is a small Hadal detail within the Progression category. It exists to support long-term progression that broadens choices instead of simply flattening danger.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **low but persistent**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **crew comparison of conflicting evidence**. The intended response is usually to **retreat before escalation**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a smell of wet iron. Experienced crews notice it earlier but may overreact.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **money and town recovery**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
