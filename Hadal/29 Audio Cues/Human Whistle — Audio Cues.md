# Human Whistle — Audio Cues

**Concept:** Human Whistle — Audio Cues is a major Hadal detail within the Audio Cues category. It exists to support sound motifs that carry actionable information while also undermining trust in that information.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **high under bad conditions**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sonar or instrument drift**. The intended response is usually to **retreat before escalation**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a smell of wet iron. It becomes more dangerous when players stop communicating.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **new dialogue and rumors**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
