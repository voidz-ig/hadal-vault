# Impossible Pulse — Audio Cues

**Concept:** Impossible Pulse — Audio Cues is a small Hadal detail within the Audio Cues category. It exists to support sound motifs that carry actionable information while also undermining trust in that information.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **high under bad conditions**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **visual observation**. The intended response is usually to **spend a limited tool charge**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a brief absence of ordinary ocean noise. It is easier to survive than to correctly understand.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **safer future routes**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
