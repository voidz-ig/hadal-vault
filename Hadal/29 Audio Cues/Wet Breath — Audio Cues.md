# Wet Breath — Audio Cues

**Concept:** Wet Breath — Audio Cues is a major Hadal detail within the Audio Cues category. It exists to support sound motifs that carry actionable information while also undermining trust in that information.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **moderate**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sound pattern recognition**. The intended response is usually to **split responsibilities**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a smell of wet iron. It is easier to survive than to correctly understand.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **rare crafting material**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
