# Signal Cycle — Diver Systems

**Concept:** Signal Cycle — Diver Systems is a major Hadal detail within the Diver Systems category. It exists to support underwater mechanics that turn descent into teamwork rather than a solo scavenging minigame.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **deceptively minor**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sonar or instrument drift**. The intended response is usually to **spend a limited tool charge**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is radio static with a human cadence. Experienced crews notice it earlier but may overreact.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **equipment access**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
