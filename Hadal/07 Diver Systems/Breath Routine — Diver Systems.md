# Breath Routine — Diver Systems

**Concept:** Breath Routine — Diver Systems is a small Hadal detail within the Diver Systems category. It exists to support underwater mechanics that turn descent into teamwork rather than a solo scavenging minigame.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **low but persistent**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **split responsibilities**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is sediment moving against the current. It can leave evidence that persists back in town.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **new dialogue and rumors**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
