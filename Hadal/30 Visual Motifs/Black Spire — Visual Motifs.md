# Black Spire — Visual Motifs

**Concept:** Black Spire — Visual Motifs is a medium Hadal detail within the Visual Motifs category. It exists to support recurring visual language used to build recognition before explicit explanation.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **low but persistent**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sonar or instrument drift**. The intended response is usually to **communicate a confirmation phrase**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is light scattering that looks slightly delayed. It is easier to survive than to correctly understand.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **rare crafting material**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
