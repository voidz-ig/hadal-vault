# White Spire — Visual Motifs

**Concept:** White Spire — Visual Motifs is a major Hadal detail within the Visual Motifs category. It exists to support recurring visual language used to build recognition before explicit explanation.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **high under bad conditions**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **communicate a confirmation phrase**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a smell of wet iron. It is easier to survive than to correctly understand.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **research value**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
