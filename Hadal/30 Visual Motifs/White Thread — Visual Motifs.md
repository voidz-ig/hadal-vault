# White Thread — Visual Motifs

**Concept:** White Thread — Visual Motifs is a small Hadal detail within the Visual Motifs category. It exists to support recurring visual language used to build recognition before explicit explanation.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **high under bad conditions**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **retreat before escalation**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is light scattering that looks slightly delayed. Its first explanation is usually mundane.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **money and town recovery**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
