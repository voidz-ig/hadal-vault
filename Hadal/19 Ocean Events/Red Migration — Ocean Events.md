# Red Migration — Ocean Events

**Concept:** Red Migration — Ocean Events is a major Hadal detail within the Ocean Events category. It exists to support run-level events that temporarily change the ocean's behavior and force plan changes.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **high under bad conditions**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **slow down and verify**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a low metallic vibration. It is easier to survive than to correctly understand.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **research value**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
