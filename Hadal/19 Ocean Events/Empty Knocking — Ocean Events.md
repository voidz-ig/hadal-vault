# Empty Knocking — Ocean Events

**Concept:** Empty Knocking — Ocean Events is a major Hadal detail within the Ocean Events category. It exists to support run-level events that temporarily change the ocean's behavior and force plan changes.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **deceptively minor**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **visual observation**. The intended response is usually to **retreat before escalation**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is sediment moving against the current. It becomes more dangerous when players stop communicating.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **safer future routes**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
