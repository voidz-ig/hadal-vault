# Structural Failure — Hazards

**Concept:** Structural Failure — Hazards is a small Hadal detail within the Hazards category. It exists to support non-creature dangers that make every site risky even before supernatural escalation.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **moderate**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **change route rather than fight**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is radio static with a human cadence. It can leave evidence that persists back in town.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **new dialogue and rumors**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
