# Acoustic Burst — Hazards

**Concept:** Acoustic Burst — Hazards is a small Hadal detail within the Hazards category. It exists to support non-creature dangers that make every site risky even before supernatural escalation.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **deceptively minor**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **visual observation**. The intended response is usually to **slow down and verify**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is light scattering that looks slightly delayed. It becomes more dangerous when players stop communicating.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **new dialogue and rumors**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
