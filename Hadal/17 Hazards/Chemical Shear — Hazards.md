# Chemical Shear — Hazards

**Concept:** Chemical Shear — Hazards is a small Hadal detail within the Hazards category. It exists to support non-creature dangers that make every site risky even before supernatural escalation.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **rare but severe**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **visual observation**. The intended response is usually to **retreat before escalation**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a low metallic vibration. Its behavior subtly changes after deeper expeditions.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **safer future routes**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
