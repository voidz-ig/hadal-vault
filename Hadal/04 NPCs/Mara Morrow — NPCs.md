# Mara Morrow — NPCs

**Concept:** Mara Morrow — NPCs is a small Hadal detail within the NPCs category. It exists to support Hollow Shores residents with routines, biases, personal stakes and imperfect knowledge of the sea.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **low but persistent**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **communicate a confirmation phrase**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is sediment moving against the current. It becomes more dangerous when players stop communicating.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **research value**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
