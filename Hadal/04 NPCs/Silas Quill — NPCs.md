# Silas Quill — NPCs

**Concept:** Silas Quill — NPCs is a small Hadal detail within the NPCs category. It exists to support Hollow Shores residents with routines, biases, personal stakes and imperfect knowledge of the sea.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **rare but severe**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **change route rather than fight**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a brief absence of ordinary ocean noise. It is easier to survive than to correctly understand.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **safer future routes**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
