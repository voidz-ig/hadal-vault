# Miriam Morrow — NPCs

**Concept:** Miriam Morrow — NPCs is a medium Hadal detail within the NPCs category. It exists to support Hollow Shores residents with routines, biases, personal stakes and imperfect knowledge of the sea.

## Gameplay role

## Progression value
