# Calder Quill — NPCs

**Concept:** Calder Quill — NPCs is a small Hadal detail within the NPCs category. It exists to support Hollow Shores residents with routines, biases, personal stakes and imperfect knowledge of the sea.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **low but persistent**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sound pattern recognition**. The intended response is usually to **spend a limited tool charge**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is radio static with a human cadence. It becomes more dangerous when players stop communicating.

## Progression value
