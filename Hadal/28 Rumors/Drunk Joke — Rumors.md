# Drunk Joke — Rumors

**Concept:** Drunk Joke — Rumors is a medium Hadal detail within the Rumors category. It exists to support small pieces of uncertain information that may foreshadow mechanics, lie, or become true later.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **low but persistent**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **change route rather than fight**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is sediment moving against the current. It is easier to survive than to correctly understand.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **rare crafting material**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
