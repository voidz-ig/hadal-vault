# Coldwater Lamp — Diving Equipment

**Concept:** Coldwater Lamp — Diving Equipment is a medium Hadal detail within the Diving Equipment category. It exists to support diver tools whose strengths create tradeoffs in weight, noise, endurance and information.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **moderate**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sound pattern recognition**. The intended response is usually to **spend a limited tool charge**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a brief absence of ordinary ocean noise. It becomes more dangerous when players stop communicating.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **money and town recovery**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
