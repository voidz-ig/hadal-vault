# Emergency Tank — Diving Equipment

**Concept:** Emergency Tank — Diving Equipment is a major Hadal detail within the Diving Equipment category. It exists to support diver tools whose strengths create tradeoffs in weight, noise, endurance and information.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **high under bad conditions**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **retreat before escalation**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a smell of wet iron. It can leave evidence that persists back in town.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **rare crafting material**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
