# Lantern Watch — Factions

**Concept:** Lantern Watch — Factions is a small Hadal detail within the Factions category. It exists to support groups competing over safety, money, research, tradition and access to recovered material.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **low but persistent**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sonar or instrument drift**. The intended response is usually to **communicate a confirmation phrase**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

A recurring tell is radio static with a human cadence. Experienced crews notice it earlier but may overreact.

## Progression value
