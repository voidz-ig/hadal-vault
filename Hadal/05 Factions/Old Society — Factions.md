# Old Society — Factions

**Concept:** Old Society — Factions is a small Hadal detail within the Factions category. It exists to support groups competing over safety, money, research, tradition and access to recovered material.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **high under bad conditions**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sonar or instrument drift**. The intended response is usually to **change route rather than fight**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is sediment moving against the current. It becomes more dangerous when players stop communicating.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **equipment access**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
