# Salt Watch — Factions

**Concept:** Salt Watch — Factions is a major Hadal detail within the Factions category. It exists to support groups competing over safety, money, research, tradition and access to recovered material.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **rare but severe**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sonar or instrument drift**. The intended response is usually to **retreat before escalation**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a brief absence of ordinary ocean noise. It is easier to survive than to correctly understand.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **rare crafting material**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
