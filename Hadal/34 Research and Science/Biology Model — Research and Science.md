# Biology Model — Research and Science

**Concept:** Biology Model — Research and Science is a small Hadal detail within the Research and Science category. It exists to support research systems that turn recovered evidence into partial understanding and new risks.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **low but persistent**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **visual observation**. The intended response is usually to **split responsibilities**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a brief absence of ordinary ocean noise. Experienced crews notice it earlier but may overreact.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **money and town recovery**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
