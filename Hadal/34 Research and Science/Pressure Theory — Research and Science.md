# Pressure Theory — Research and Science

**Concept:** Pressure Theory — Research and Science is a medium Hadal detail within the Research and Science category. It exists to support research systems that turn recovered evidence into partial understanding and new risks.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **deceptively minor**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sound pattern recognition**. The intended response is usually to **change route rather than fight**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a smell of wet iron. It can leave evidence that persists back in town.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **new dialogue and rumors**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
