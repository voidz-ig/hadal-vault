# Primary Radar — Ship Systems

**Concept:** Primary Radar — Ship Systems is a major Hadal detail within the Ship Systems category. It exists to support ship machinery sailors monitor, repair, reroute and distrust under pressure.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **deceptively minor**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sonar or instrument drift**. The intended response is usually to **change route rather than fight**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is radio static with a human cadence. Its behavior subtly changes after deeper expeditions.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **safer future routes**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
