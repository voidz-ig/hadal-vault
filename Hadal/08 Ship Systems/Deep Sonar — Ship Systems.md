# Deep Sonar — Ship Systems

**Concept:** Deep Sonar — Ship Systems is a small Hadal detail within the Ship Systems category. It exists to support ship machinery sailors monitor, repair, reroute and distrust under pressure.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **rare but severe**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **crew comparison of conflicting evidence**. The intended response is usually to **spend a limited tool charge**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a brief absence of ordinary ocean noise. Experienced crews notice it earlier but may overreact.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **new dialogue and rumors**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
