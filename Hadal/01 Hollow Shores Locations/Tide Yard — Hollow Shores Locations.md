# Tide Yard — Hollow Shores Locations

**Concept:** Tide Yard — Hollow Shores Locations is a major Hadal detail within the Hollow Shores Locations category. It exists to support physical places inside Hollow Shores that make the town feel lived-in and economically strained.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **rare but severe**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **spend a limited tool charge**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is light scattering that looks slightly delayed. It becomes more dangerous when players stop communicating.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **money and town recovery**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
