# Gull Warehouse — Hollow Shores Locations

**Concept:** Gull Warehouse — Hollow Shores Locations is a small Hadal detail within the Hollow Shores Locations category. It exists to support physical places inside Hollow Shores that make the town feel lived-in and economically strained.
