# Diver's Lens — Artifacts

**Concept:** Diver's Lens — Artifacts is a small Hadal detail within the Artifacts category. It exists to support rare objects whose mechanical usefulness is inseparable from disturbing provenance.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **deceptively minor**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sound pattern recognition**. The intended response is usually to **change route rather than fight**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is radio static with a human cadence. It is easier to survive than to correctly understand.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **rare crafting material**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
