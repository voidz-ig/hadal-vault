# Black Key — Artifacts

**Concept:** Black Key — Artifacts is a small Hadal detail within the Artifacts category. It exists to support rare objects whose mechanical usefulness is inseparable from disturbing provenance.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **rare but severe**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sound pattern recognition**. The intended response is usually to **retreat before escalation**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a smell of wet iron. It becomes more dangerous when players stop communicating.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **safer future routes**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
