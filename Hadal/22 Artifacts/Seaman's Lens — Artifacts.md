# Seaman's Lens — Artifacts

**Concept:** Seaman's Lens — Artifacts is a small Hadal detail within the Artifacts category. It exists to support rare objects whose mechanical usefulness is inseparable from disturbing provenance.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **low but persistent**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **split responsibilities**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a low metallic vibration. It can leave evidence that persists back in town.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **equipment access**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
