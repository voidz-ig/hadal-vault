# Iron Beacon — Town Infrastructure

**Concept:** Iron Beacon — Town Infrastructure is a major Hadal detail within the Town Infrastructure category. It exists to support infrastructure whose condition affects preparation, safety, navigation or atmosphere.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **rare but severe**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **spend a limited tool charge**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a smell of wet iron. Experienced crews notice it earlier but may overreact.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **rare crafting material**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
