# Lower Crane — Town Infrastructure

**Concept:** Lower Crane — Town Infrastructure is a major Hadal detail within the Town Infrastructure category. It exists to support infrastructure whose condition affects preparation, safety, navigation or atmosphere.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **deceptively minor**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sound pattern recognition**. The intended response is usually to **spend a limited tool charge**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is radio static with a human cadence. It can leave evidence that persists back in town.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **rare crafting material**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
