# Long-Range Winch — Ship Upgrades

**Concept:** Long-Range Winch — Ship Upgrades is a medium Hadal detail within the Ship Upgrades category. It exists to support persistent upgrades that change expedition capability without removing uncertainty.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **rare but severe**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **retreat before escalation**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is sediment moving against the current. Its behavior subtly changes after deeper expeditions.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **money and town recovery**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
