# Salvage Locker — Ship Upgrades

**Concept:** Salvage Locker — Ship Upgrades is a small Hadal detail within the Ship Upgrades category. It exists to support persistent upgrades that change expedition capability without removing uncertainty.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **low but persistent**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sound pattern recognition**. The intended response is usually to **retreat before escalation**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a smell of wet iron. It can leave evidence that persists back in town.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **new dialogue and rumors**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
