# Long-Range Console — Ship Upgrades

**Concept:** Long-Range Console — Ship Upgrades is a small Hadal detail within the Ship Upgrades category. It exists to support persistent upgrades that change expedition capability without removing uncertainty.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **deceptively minor**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **slow down and verify**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a low metallic vibration. Its first explanation is usually mundane.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **equipment access**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
