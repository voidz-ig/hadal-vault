# Research Locker — Ship Upgrades

**Concept:** Research Locker — Ship Upgrades is a major Hadal detail within the Ship Upgrades category. It exists to support persistent upgrades that change expedition capability without removing uncertainty.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **moderate**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **change route rather than fight**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is light scattering that looks slightly delayed. It becomes more dangerous when players stop communicating.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **rare crafting material**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
