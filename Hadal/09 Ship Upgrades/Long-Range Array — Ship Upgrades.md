# Long-Range Array — Ship Upgrades

**Concept:** Long-Range Array — Ship Upgrades is a major Hadal detail within the Ship Upgrades category. It exists to support persistent upgrades that change expedition capability without removing uncertainty.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **low but persistent**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sound pattern recognition**. The intended response is usually to **spend a limited tool charge**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a smell of wet iron. Its behavior subtly changes after deeper expeditions.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **new dialogue and rumors**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
