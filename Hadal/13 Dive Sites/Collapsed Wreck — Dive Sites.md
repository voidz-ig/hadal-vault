# Collapsed Wreck — Dive Sites

**Concept:** Collapsed Wreck — Dive Sites is a major Hadal detail within the Dive Sites category. It exists to support specific expedition destinations that combine resources, hazards and environmental storytelling.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **low but persistent**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sonar or instrument drift**. The intended response is usually to **change route rather than fight**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is sediment moving against the current. Its behavior subtly changes after deeper expeditions.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **money and town recovery**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
