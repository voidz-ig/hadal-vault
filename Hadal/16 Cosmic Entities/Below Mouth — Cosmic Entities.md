# Below Mouth — Cosmic Entities

**Concept:** Below Mouth — Cosmic Entities is a small Hadal detail within the Cosmic Entities category. It exists to support late-game phenomena that attack assumptions about scale, identity, distance and cause.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **high under bad conditions**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **visual observation**. The intended response is usually to **slow down and verify**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is light scattering that looks slightly delayed. It can leave evidence that persists back in town.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **rare crafting material**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
