# Last Choir — Cosmic Entities

**Concept:** Last Choir — Cosmic Entities is a medium Hadal detail within the Cosmic Entities category. It exists to support late-game phenomena that attack assumptions about scale, identity, distance and cause.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **rare but severe**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sound pattern recognition**. The intended response is usually to **retreat before escalation**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a smell of wet iron. It can leave evidence that persists back in town.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **new dialogue and rumors**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
