# Hollow Shape — Cosmic Entities

**Concept:** Hollow Shape — Cosmic Entities is a small Hadal detail within the Cosmic Entities category. It exists to support late-game phenomena that attack assumptions about scale, identity, distance and cause.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **low but persistent**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sonar or instrument drift**. The intended response is usually to **change route rather than fight**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a low metallic vibration. Its behavior subtly changes after deeper expeditions.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **rare crafting material**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
