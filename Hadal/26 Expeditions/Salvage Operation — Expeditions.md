# Salvage Operation — Expeditions

**Concept:** Salvage Operation — Expeditions is a small Hadal detail within the Expeditions category. It exists to support repeatable expedition formats with different risk, role pressure and extraction priorities.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **rare but severe**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sonar or instrument drift**. The intended response is usually to **split responsibilities**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **rare crafting material**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
