# Research Dive — Expeditions

**Concept:** Research Dive — Expeditions is a medium Hadal detail within the Expeditions category. It exists to support repeatable expedition formats with different risk, role pressure and extraction priorities.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **deceptively minor**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **split responsibilities**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a low metallic vibration. It can leave evidence that persists back in town.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **research value**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
