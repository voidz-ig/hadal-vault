# Restricted Route — Expeditions

**Concept:** Restricted Route — Expeditions is a small Hadal detail within the Expeditions category. It exists to support repeatable expedition formats with different risk, role pressure and extraction priorities.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **moderate**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **change route rather than fight**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is light scattering that looks slightly delayed. Its behavior subtly changes after deeper expeditions.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **equipment access**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
