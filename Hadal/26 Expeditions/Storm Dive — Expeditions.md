# Storm Dive — Expeditions

**Concept:** Storm Dive — Expeditions is a small Hadal detail within the Expeditions category. It exists to support repeatable expedition formats with different risk, role pressure and extraction priorities.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **high under bad conditions**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **visual observation**. The intended response is usually to **change route rather than fight**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a low metallic vibration. Experienced crews notice it earlier but may overreact.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **new dialogue and rumors**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
