# Forbidden Delivery — Quests

**Concept:** Forbidden Delivery — Quests is a small Hadal detail within the Quests category. It exists to support missions grounded in local needs that gradually pull players toward stranger explanations.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **low but persistent**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sonar or instrument drift**. The intended response is usually to **slow down and verify**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is light scattering that looks slightly delayed. Experienced crews notice it earlier but may overreact.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **money and town recovery**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
