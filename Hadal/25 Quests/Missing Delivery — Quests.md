# Missing Delivery — Quests

**Concept:** Missing Delivery — Quests is a major Hadal detail within the Quests category. It exists to support missions grounded in local needs that gradually pull players toward stranger explanations.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **rare but severe**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **slow down and verify**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is radio static with a human cadence. It becomes more dangerous when players stop communicating.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **money and town recovery**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
