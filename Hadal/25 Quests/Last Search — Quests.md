# Last Search — Quests

**Concept:** Last Search — Quests is a major Hadal detail within the Quests category. It exists to support missions grounded in local needs that gradually pull players toward stranger explanations.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **high under bad conditions**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **crew comparison of conflicting evidence**. The intended response is usually to **change route rather than fight**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is radio static with a human cadence. It is easier to survive than to correctly understand.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **research value**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
