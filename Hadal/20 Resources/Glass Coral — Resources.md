# Glass Coral — Resources

**Concept:** Glass Coral — Resources is a small Hadal detail within the Resources category. It exists to support materials gathered for sale, crafting, town recovery, research and morally questionable experiments.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **moderate**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sonar or instrument drift**. The intended response is usually to **spend a limited tool charge**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a brief absence of ordinary ocean noise. Experienced crews notice it earlier but may overreact.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **rare crafting material**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
