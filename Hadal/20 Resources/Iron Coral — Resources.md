# Iron Coral — Resources

**Concept:** Iron Coral — Resources is a small Hadal detail within the Resources category. It exists to support materials gathered for sale, crafting, town recovery, research and morally questionable experiments.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **deceptively minor**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **crew comparison of conflicting evidence**. The intended response is usually to **slow down and verify**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a brief absence of ordinary ocean noise. It becomes more dangerous when players stop communicating.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **new dialogue and rumors**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
