# Panic Exposure — Status Effects

**Concept:** Panic Exposure — Status Effects is a small Hadal detail within the Status Effects category. It exists to support temporary conditions that alter decisions, perception or coordination rather than only draining health.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **deceptively minor**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **visual observation**. The intended response is usually to **change route rather than fight**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a smell of wet iron. It is easier to survive than to correctly understand.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **safer future routes**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
