# Panic Distortion — Status Effects

**Concept:** Panic Distortion — Status Effects is a medium Hadal detail within the Status Effects category. It exists to support temporary conditions that alter decisions, perception or coordination rather than only draining health.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **deceptively minor**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sound pattern recognition**. The intended response is usually to **split responsibilities**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a low metallic vibration. It can leave evidence that persists back in town.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **safer future routes**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
