# Pale Shelf — Ocean Regions

**Concept:** Pale Shelf — Ocean Regions is a small Hadal detail within the Ocean Regions category. It exists to support large marine regions with distinct ecology, navigation rules and horror language.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **high under bad conditions**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sonar or instrument drift**. The intended response is usually to **slow down and verify**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is radio static with a human cadence. It is easier to survive than to correctly understand.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **money and town recovery**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
