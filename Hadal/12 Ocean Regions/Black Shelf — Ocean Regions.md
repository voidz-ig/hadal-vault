# Black Shelf — Ocean Regions

**Concept:** Black Shelf — Ocean Regions is a small Hadal detail within the Ocean Regions category. It exists to support large marine regions with distinct ecology, navigation rules and horror language.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **rare but severe**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sound pattern recognition**. The intended response is usually to **slow down and verify**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a brief absence of ordinary ocean noise. It becomes more dangerous when players stop communicating.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **safer future routes**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
