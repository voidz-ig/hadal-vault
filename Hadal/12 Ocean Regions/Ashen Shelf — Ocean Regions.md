# Ashen Shelf — Ocean Regions

**Concept:** Ashen Shelf — Ocean Regions is a major Hadal detail within the Ocean Regions category. It exists to support large marine regions with distinct ecology, navigation rules and horror language.

Its baseline pressure is **moderate**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **slow down and verify**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Progression value
