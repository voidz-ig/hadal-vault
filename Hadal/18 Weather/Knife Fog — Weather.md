# Knife Fog — Weather

**Concept:** Knife Fog — Weather is a major Hadal detail within the Weather category. It exists to support surface conditions that change visibility, handling, communication and threat readability.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **low but persistent**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **crew comparison of conflicting evidence**. The intended response is usually to **spend a limited tool charge**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a brief absence of ordinary ocean noise. It can leave evidence that persists back in town.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **equipment access**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
