# Iron Sea — Weather

**Concept:** Iron Sea — Weather is a small Hadal detail within the Weather category. It exists to support surface conditions that change visibility, handling, communication and threat readability.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **deceptively minor**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **visual observation**. The intended response is usually to **change route rather than fight**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is sediment moving against the current. Experienced crews notice it earlier but may overreact.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **equipment access**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
