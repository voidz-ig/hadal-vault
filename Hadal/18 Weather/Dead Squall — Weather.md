# Dead Squall — Weather

**Concept:** Dead Squall — Weather is a small Hadal detail within the Weather category. It exists to support surface conditions that change visibility, handling, communication and threat readability.

Its baseline pressure is **low but persistent**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sonar or instrument drift**. The intended response is usually to **spend a limited tool charge**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

A recurring tell is a low metallic vibration. It is easier to survive than to correctly understand.

## Progression value
