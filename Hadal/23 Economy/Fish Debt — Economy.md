# Fish Debt — Economy

**Concept:** Fish Debt — Economy is a small Hadal detail within the Economy category. It exists to support economic rules that keep mundane pressure relevant beside supernatural stakes.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **moderate**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **radio behavior**. The intended response is usually to **communicate a confirmation phrase**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a smell of wet iron. It becomes more dangerous when players stop communicating.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **equipment access**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
