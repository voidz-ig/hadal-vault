# Research Debt — Economy

**Concept:** Research Debt — Economy is a small Hadal detail within the Economy category. It exists to support economic rules that keep mundane pressure relevant beside supernatural stakes.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **high under bad conditions**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sonar or instrument drift**. The intended response is usually to **slow down and verify**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is sediment moving against the current. It becomes more dangerous when players stop communicating.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **new dialogue and rumors**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
