# Radio Cycle — Sailor Systems

**Concept:** Radio Cycle — Sailor Systems is a major Hadal detail within the Sailor Systems category. It exists to support surface gameplay tasks that keep sailors active and make their judgment matter.

Its baseline pressure is **moderate**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **visual observation**. The intended response is usually to **slow down and verify**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **new dialogue and rumors**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
