# Watch Routine — Sailor Systems

**Concept:** Watch Routine — Sailor Systems is a major Hadal detail within the Sailor Systems category. It exists to support surface gameplay tasks that keep sailors active and make their judgment matter.

## Gameplay role
Its baseline pressure is **deceptively minor**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sound pattern recognition**. The intended response is usually to **spend a limited tool charge**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Sensory identity
A recurring tell is a smell of wet iron. It becomes more dangerous when players stop communicating.

## Progression value
Interacting with, surviving, repairing, studying or exploiting this detail can contribute to **research value**. Deeper versions should connect back to [[Depth Escalation]] and become less certain rather than simply gaining more health.
