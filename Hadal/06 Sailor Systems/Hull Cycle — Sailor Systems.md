# Hull Cycle — Sailor Systems

**Concept:** Hull Cycle — Sailor Systems is a small Hadal detail within the Sailor Systems category. It exists to support surface gameplay tasks that keep sailors active and make their judgment matter.

Its baseline pressure is **low but persistent**. Players most reliably identify or understand it through **sound pattern recognition**. The intended response is usually to **split responsibilities**, rather than treating every problem as combat.

## Progression value
